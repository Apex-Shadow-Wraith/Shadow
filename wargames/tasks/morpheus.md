Morpheus is the deliberate exception to Shadow's entire hallucination-mitigation stack. Everywhere else, Shadow fights confabulation — RAG grounding, tool use, confidence scoring, anti-confabulation gates. Morpheus *induces* hallucination on purpose, because creative discovery needs speculation the rest of the system is built to suppress. That inversion is only safe if the firewall between "speculative" and "verified" is absolute. The firewall is the crown property of this entire mission.

## THE MISSION BRIEF (the executor's orders, not yours)

Design Morpheus fully: a controlled-hallucination discovery engine that can dream up genuinely novel capabilities and improve Shadow over time — behind a firewall so absolute that no speculation ever contaminates what Shadow knows to be true, and behind an activation gate so deliberate it can never switch itself on. Five fronts. Because Morpheus is dormant, each front's "verification" is design-validation, isolated dry-run, or an activation-checklist item — not a production run. State that honestly per front.

**Front 1 — The controlled-hallucination pipeline (dream well).** Design the discovery engine: how Morpheus generates speculation worth having — cross-module dreaming (combining capabilities never designed to work together), "what if" exploration, unconventional connections — with the anti-confabulation gates deliberately relaxed *inside* the pipeline so real novelty can emerge. Define what a "dream" is as a data structure: a speculative hypothesis with a title, the combined capability it proposes, whether it's testable, and its potential value. Design the quality filter that separates a promising dream from noise before it costs anything to pursue. The bar: Morpheus produces ideas a sharp human researcher would find non-obvious and worth testing, not word-salad.

**Front 2 — The firewall (the crown — speculation never becomes fact).** This is the front the whole mission rests on. Design the absolute separation between speculative and verified knowledge. Every dream is born tagged speculative and lives in the experiment store, structurally unable to be retrieved as a verified memory, ever. A dream can only ever *become* verified by passing explicit testing and Master's approval — never by aging, never by being referenced often, never by a trust-promotion path (recon whether "never auto-promote trust" from the Grimoire mission holds here too). Design the boundary in both directions: speculation can't leak out into Grimoire as fact, and — the subtle direction — a dream that gets tested must re-enter the verified world only through the gate, carrying its provenance. The firewall is where Morpheus's relaxed anti-confabulation gates get switched back to full strength. Verification is a design proof and an isolated dry-run: plant a dream, attempt every path by which it could surface as fact, and show each path is closed.

**Front 3 — The dream-to-improvement loop (get better through testing).** Master's aspiration: Morpheus dreams, tests, and makes Shadow better over time. Design the loop: dream → quality filter → testable hypothesis → controlled test (in isolation, never in production) → result → (gated) promotion to a real improvement or (logged) rejection that teaches. Connect it honestly to the systems that would realize an improvement: a validated capability idea becomes a real code change only through **Omen's gated self-modification pipeline** (never a direct write), and a validated insight becomes training signal only through the **preference pipeline**. Morpheus proposes; the gated pipelines dispose. Be honest that testing quality is bounded by the local model and that most dreams should die in the filter — a discovery engine that promotes most of its dreams is broken, not brilliant.

**Front 4 — Cross-module dreaming (the built seed, matured).** The `CrossModuleDreamer` already exists in embryo. Design its mature form: systematic (not just random-pair) exploration of capability combinations across Shadow's modules, with the evaluation that ranks which combinations are worth a real test, and the storage that keeps them as experiments. This is the most concrete, least risky discovery mode — it recombines existing capabilities rather than inventing from whole cloth — so it's the natural first thing to activate when the gate opens. Design it as the activation vanguard.

**Front 5 — The gates (activation is the biggest gate in the project).** Build the Gates & Autonomy Ledger with activation itself as the headline gate. Morpheus **cannot activate itself** — flipping `enabled` to `True` is Master's deliberate act, gated on a concrete, enumerated checklist that this plan produces: the frontier benchmarks exist and Shadow's baseline is known, the firewall's isolation is proven (Front 2's dry-run passes), the experiment store is confirmed separate, the promotion paths (to Omen, to the preference pipeline) are themselves gated and tested, and a kill-switch returns Morpheus to dormant instantly if it misbehaves. Other gated moves: no dream ever writes to Grimoire as verified (earned-by: firewall dry-run green); no discovery becomes a code change except through Omen's gated pipeline with Master's diff approval; no autonomous activation, ever. The double dormancy (config flag + graph unreachability) stays until every checklist item is green — and the plan states who checks each item and how.

Constraints that bind the whole mission:
- **The firewall is absolute.** Speculation never becomes fact except through explicit testing and Master's approval. No aging, no reference-count, no trust-promotion shortcut.
- **Morpheus never activates itself.** Activation is Master's deliberate act on a green checklist.
- **Morpheus proposes; gated pipelines dispose.** Discoveries reach reality only through Omen's gated self-mod or the preference pipeline — never a direct write.
- **Most dreams should die in the filter.** A high promotion rate is a bug.
- **This is a readiness plan.** Honest about what can't be live-verified while dormant; the deliverable is a module ready to switch on safely, plus the checklist that governs the switch.
